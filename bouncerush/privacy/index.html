Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Bounce Rush.

Last updated: July 23, 2026
Bounce Rush does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone access. The game displays advertising through Google AdMob.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Bounce Rush, published by AL Games Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). By using the game, you acknowledge the information practices described below.

2. Information we handle

We do not operate user accounts or directly request personal information from you. We do not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in Bounce Rush.

Bounce Rush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Depending on your device, settings, consent choices, region, and the ads served, Google AdMob may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, including an advertising identifier when available;
  • advertising data, such as ads viewed or interacted with;
  • app interactions, such as launches, taps, or video views;
  • diagnostic, crash, and performance information; and
  • device and technical information needed to deliver ads.

We do not receive information that you enter into an account because Bounce Rush has no account or sign-in system.

3. How information is used

Information processed through Google AdMob may be used to:

  • deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements;
  • limit repeated ads and improve advertising performance;
  • detect fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • maintain and improve the advertising service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Whether ads are personalized may depend on your region, age, consent choices, and device privacy settings.

4. Advertising partner

Google AdMob is an independent third-party service provider. Google processes information under its applicable terms and privacy practices. We only use service providers whose applicable terms and public commitments require safeguards that are consistent with or equivalent to the protections described in this policy and required by applicable law. We remain responsible for reviewing the privacy practices of third-party SDKs included in the game.

You can learn more about Google’s practices at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If we add another advertising or analytics provider, we will review its privacy and security commitments and update this policy before or when that service is introduced.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

When a consent message is required in your region, you can withdraw or change your advertising consent using the Privacy Choices control available from the game’s settings or privacy menu. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

You can also revoke iOS tracking permission at any time by opening Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device and disabling permission for Bounce Rush. Ads may still be shown, but the advertising identifier will not be available for tracking after permission is denied or revoked.

You can manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center and manage information associated with a Google Account through Google Data & Privacy .

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. To exercise these rights or request deletion, email alimirzayef@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law and, when information is controlled solely by Google, help direct your request to the appropriate Google process. We may ask for limited information necessary to understand and verify your request.

Because Bounce Rush has no user accounts and we do not maintain player profiles, we may not possess information that can be directly identified as yours.

6. Children’s privacy

Bounce Rush is not directed to children under 13, or to a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Data retention, deletion, security, and transfers

We do not maintain a database of Bounce Rush user accounts or player profiles. If you contact us, we keep the correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. We then delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed.

Google determines retention periods for information processed by AdMob according to the type of data, the purpose for which it was collected, and legal, security, fraud-prevention, and service continuity requirements. Google describes these periods and its deletion process in How Google retains data . A verified deletion request will be handled under our applicable obligations and the relevant provider’s deletion procedures.

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable legal safeguards. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our providers use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information.

8. Policy page hosting

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit this webpage, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address for security and service operation purposes under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates when the latest revision took effect.
