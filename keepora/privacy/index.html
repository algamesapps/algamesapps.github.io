Keepora

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Keepora handles information when you save, organize, and revisit links shared from other apps.

Last updated: August 1, 2026
Keepora does not require an account, show advertising, use analytics, or upload your saved library to servers operated by AL Games Apps. Your saves are stored locally on your device.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Keepora mobile application and its iOS Share Extension, published by AL Games Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Keepora lets you save and organize links shared from apps and websites, then return to them later.

2. Information Keepora processes

Keepora processes information you choose to save and information needed to provide its link-management features, including:

  • links and text you explicitly share or add to Keepora;
  • titles, notes, collection names, and collection choices you create;
  • public metadata associated with a saved link, such as a page title, description, source, location label, or thumbnail; and
  • local preferences, onboarding status, and recent search terms.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or social-media login to use Keepora.

3. Local storage and the Share Extension

Your Keepora library and preferences are stored locally on your device. On iOS, Keepora and its Share Extension use a private App Group container so a link saved through the system share sheet can appear in the main app. Keepora does not upload your library to a Keepora account or an AL Games Apps cloud database.

Device backups managed by you or your platform provider may include app data according to the backup settings and privacy terms of that provider.

4. Link metadata and network requests

To retrieve a public title, description, source label, or thumbnail, Keepora may request the link you saved or a public metadata endpoint operated by the source service. For example, a TikTok link may use TikTok’s public embed metadata service. As with a normal web request, the destination service may receive technical information such as your IP address, network information, and request time under its own privacy policy.

5. Embedded content and external services

When you preview, play, or open a saved item, Keepora may load a webpage or media embed directly from Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Facebook, YouTube, Google, or another source selected by you. These services operate independently and may process device, network, cookie, account, or usage information under their own privacy policies. Keepora does not control their data practices.

We do not sell your information or provide your saved library to those services. A connection occurs only as needed to retrieve or display content that you choose to save, preview, or open. Any service provider that processes information on our behalf must protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law.

6. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Keepora does not include advertising networks, AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or other analytics SDKs. We do not use your information for advertising, sell personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

7. Device permissions

Keepora does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library. A location label shown for a saved item is public metadata from that item; it is not your device location. Keepora also does not use Game Center or offer in-app purchases.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Saved items, collections, preferences, and recent searches remain locally on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s data, or uninstall Keepora. You can delete individual saves and collections inside the app.

Because Keepora has no user account and we do not keep a server-side copy of your library, there is normally no library data for us to access or delete. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of any correspondence you sent us, email alimirzayef@gmail.com. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, keep appropriate records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

9. Security

Keepora relies on iOS application sandboxing and App Group access controls to separate its local data from unrelated apps. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children

Keepora is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

11. Policy page hosting

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit this webpage, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address for security and service operation purposes under GitHub’s Privacy Statement .

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Keepora, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected here before or when they take effect.

13. Contact us

Contact AL Games Apps with privacy questions or requests at alimirzayef@gmail.com.
