Numbo – Math Kids

Children’s Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in our child-directed math learning game.

Last updated: July 26, 2026
Numbo – Math Kids does not require an account and does not ask children for a name, email address, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone access. Advertising is configured for child-directed treatment and limited to non-personalized, age-appropriate contextual ads.

1. About this policy

This Children’s Privacy Policy applies to Numbo – Math Kids, published by AL Games Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). The game is designed for children and families. This policy is intended to help parents and guardians understand how information is handled.

2. Information we do not collect directly

We do not operate user accounts and do not ask a child to provide personal information. The game does not directly collect a child’s:

  • name, email address, phone number, or home address;
  • precise location;
  • photos, videos, drawings, camera, or microphone recordings;
  • contacts or messages;
  • date of birth or school information; or
  • account credentials.

We do not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics, and the game does not include a user account, Game Center, or in-app purchases.

3. Information stored only on the device

The game stores the selected grade level, game progress, and sound preference locally on the device so the child can continue playing later. This information is not sent to our servers and is not used to identify the child. It remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled.

4. Child-directed advertising

Numbo – Math Kids uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. All ad requests are configured for child age treatment and a maximum G content rating.

With child age treatment enabled, Google states that:

  • personalized and interest-based advertising is disabled;
  • remarketing is disabled;
  • the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is not transmitted;
  • requests to third-party ad vendors are restricted; and
  • additional child ad-serving protections are applied.

Google may still process limited technical information necessary to deliver contextual ads, prevent fraud, maintain security, and operate the advertising service. Depending on Google’s current SDK behavior, this may include an IP address, basic device or app information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. We do not use this information to build profiles of children or to serve personalized ads.

5. Advertising provider and safeguards

Google AdMob is an independent third-party service provider. We configure AdMob for child-directed, non-personalized advertising and use service providers whose applicable terms and public commitments require safeguards consistent with this policy and applicable children’s privacy laws.

You can learn more about Google’s practices at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google child age treatment

We review the privacy practices of third-party SDKs included in the game. If another advertising or analytics provider is introduced, we will review its child privacy and security commitments and update this policy before or when the service is added.

6. Parents’ and guardians’ rights

A parent or guardian may contact us to ask whether we hold personal information about their child, request access or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. Email alimirzayef@gmail.com.

Because the game has no accounts and we do not maintain child profiles or a player database, we generally will not have information that can be directly identified as belonging to a particular child. If information is controlled solely by Google, we will help direct the request to the appropriate Google process. We may ask for limited information necessary to understand and verify a parental request.

7. Data retention and deletion

Grade level, game progress, and sound preference remain locally on the device until the app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. We do not retain a server-side copy.

If a parent or guardian contacts us, we keep the correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. We then delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed.

Google determines retention periods for information processed by AdMob according to the type of data, its purpose, and legal, security, fraud-prevention, and service-continuity requirements. Google describes its retention and deletion process in How Google retains data .

8. Security and international processing

We and our providers use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Google may process limited technical information in countries other than the user’s own, subject to its applicable legal safeguards.

9. Policy page hosting

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a parent or guardian visits this webpage, GitHub may process technical information such as the visitor’s IP address for security and service operation purposes under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, advertising service, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates when the latest revision took effect. Material changes to how children’s information is handled will be reflected here before or when they take effect.

11. Contact us

Parents and guardians can contact AL Games Apps with questions, concerns, or requests at alimirzayef@gmail.com.
