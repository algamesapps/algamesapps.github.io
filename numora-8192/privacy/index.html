This policy explains how information is handled when you play Numora – 8192.

Last updated: August 2, 2026
Numora – 8192 does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone access. The game displays advertising through Google AdMob.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Numora – 8192, published by AL Games Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). By using the game, you acknowledge the information practices described below.

2. Information we handle

We do not operate user accounts or directly ask you to provide personal information. Numora – 8192 does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Numora – 8192 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on your device, settings, region, SDK version, and the ads served, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, including an advertising identifier when available and permitted;
  • advertising data, such as ads viewed or interacted with;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps, or video views;
  • diagnostic, crash, and performance information; and
  • device and technical information needed to deliver ads.

The game does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library. An approximate location inferred by AdMob from an IP address is different from device location access.

3. How information is used

Information processed through Google AdMob may be used to:

  • deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements;
  • limit repeated ads and improve advertising performance;
  • detect fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • maintain and improve the advertising service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Whether ads are personalized may depend on your region, consent, age, device privacy settings, and the game’s advertising configuration.

4. Advertising provider and sharing

Google AdMob is an independent third-party service provider. Information described above may be transmitted to and processed by Google and advertising partners involved in delivering, measuring, securing, or improving ads. Google handles this information under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers whose applicable terms and public commitments require protections consistent with or equivalent to this policy and applicable law, and we review the privacy practices of third-party SDKs included in the game.

You can learn more about Google’s practices at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can manage app tracking permissions in your device settings and manage Google advertising preferences through Google My Ad Center. The choices available to you may vary by device, region, and whether you use a Google Account.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. To make a request, email alimirzayef@gmail.com. When information is controlled solely by Google, we will help direct you to the appropriate Google process. We may ask for limited information needed to understand and verify your request.

Because Numora – 8192 has no accounts and we do not maintain player profiles, we may not possess information that can be directly identified as yours.

6. Other game features and permissions

Numora – 8192 does not use Game Center, offer in-app purchases, or require a user account. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo library.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, contact us so we can review the matter.

Providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable legal safeguards. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, but we and our providers use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information.
