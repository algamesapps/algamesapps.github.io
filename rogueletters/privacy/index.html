Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Rogue Letters.

Last updated: July 23, 2026
Rogue Letters does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, photos, camera, or microphone access. The game displays advertising through Google AdMob.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Rogue Letters, published by AL Games Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”). By using the game, you acknowledge the information practices described below.

2. Information we handle

We do not operate user accounts or directly request personal information from you. We do not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in Rogue Letters.

Rogue Letters uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Depending on your device, settings, consent choices, region, and the ads served, Google AdMob may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, including an advertising identifier when available;
  • advertising data, such as ads viewed or interacted with;
  • app interactions, such as launches, taps, or video views;
  • diagnostic, crash, and performance information; and
  • device and technical information needed to deliver ads.

We do not receive information that you enter into an account because Rogue Letters has no account or sign-in system.

3. How information is used

Information processed through Google AdMob may be used to:

  • deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements;
  • limit repeated ads and improve advertising performance;
  • detect fraud, abuse, and security issues;
  • maintain and improve the advertising service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Whether ads are personalized may depend on your region, age, consent choices, and device privacy settings.

4. Advertising partner

Google AdMob is an independent third-party service. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy practices. You can learn more at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If additional advertising networks are added in the future, this policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced.

5. Your privacy choices

You may limit ad personalization through your iOS privacy and tracking settings. Where required, you may also be shown a consent message that allows you to manage advertising choices. Google provides additional controls through its ad and privacy settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. Contact us using the email below to make a request. Because we do not maintain user accounts, we may not hold information that can be directly linked to you.

6. Children’s privacy

Rogue Letters is not directed to children under 13, or to a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Data retention, security, and transfers

We do not maintain a database of Rogue Letters user accounts or player profiles. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained and protected according to Google’s policies. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their applicable legal safeguards.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our providers use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information.

8. Policy page hosting

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit this webpage, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address for security and service operation purposes under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates when the latest revision took effect.
